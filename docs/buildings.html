Buildings

The Swiss building stock: what it is made of, how it is heated, how efficient it is, how fast it is being renovated, and the life-cycle impact of the materials used. The data overlaps with the inputs of building-stock models such as CESAR-P.

Building stock

Energy performance

National intensities come from the cantons' harmonised reporting. Per-building values are either modelled (FOEN, all residential buildings) or measured / certified in individual cantons (Geneva IDC, Lucerne GEAK). There is no open national dataset of energy performance certificates.

Retrofit and heating replacement

There is no official annual renovation-rate series. The SFOE/TEP Energy study estimates envelope renovation at about 1.5 % of the stock per year (2011–2020, see Library). Life-cycle impact of building materials

The KBOB/ecobau/IPB list is the Swiss reference for embodied impacts (grey energy, greenhouse gases, UBP) and is used by SIA 2032, SIA 390/1 and CESAR-P. Values here are per kg. For construction methods, compare whole elements per m² with the same function, for example with the Bauteilkatalog.

Link to CESAR-P

CESAR-P (City Energy Simulation And Retrofit Platform, Empa, AGPL-3.0, Orehounig et al. 2022) simulates the hourly energy demand of building stocks with EnergyPlus and evaluates retrofit scenarios with costs and embodied emissions. Its inputs and their open counterparts:

What is not openly available

  • Energy performance certificates: no national open GEAK/CECB data. About 207,000 buildings are certified (GEAK), but only Lucerne (since 2019) and Jura (91 public buildings) publish results.
  • Renovation rate: no annual series. The GWR has no renovation year (the variable was last collected in the 2000 census). The Building Programme only covers subsidised projects, and FSO "Umbau" investment is not energy-specific.
  • Measured energy use per building: open only in Geneva. The FOEN per-building CO2 values are modelled from GWR data that is often outdated.
  • Embodied emissions of the stock: no official dataset. KBOB gives per-material factors; stock totals exist only in research models (see Library → Models).
  • Norms: SIA 2024, 380/1, 2032, 2040 and 390/1 are paid standards.
  • Minergie: the open dataset (~33,000 buildings) is a snapshot and smaller than Minergie's own total (~60,500).
