Heat

How heat is supplied beyond individual boilers and heat pumps. District heating is still a small share of heat supply, but it is growing fast, fed mainly by waste incineration and wood.

District heating

The energy balance counts district heat once it reaches consumers. The SFOE register of thermal networks lists about 1,700 networks reported by their operators, from small wood-chip networks in villages to city networks fed by waste incineration. About 6 % of buildings name district heating as their main heat source (building register, see Buildings).
