National energy balance

How much energy Switzerland consumes, in which form, and in which sectors, and the greenhouse gases it emits.

Greenhouse gas emissions

From the national greenhouse gas inventory (FOEN). Emissions fell by about a quarter between 1990 and 2024, mostly in buildings, as oil heating is replaced. Transport is now the largest sector. International aviation, about 5.5 Mt CO2-eq in 2024, is reported separately and not included here.
