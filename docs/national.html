National energy balance

Where Switzerland's energy comes from, how much of it is imported, in which form and in which sectors it is used, and the greenhouse gases it emits.

Energy supply and import dependency

Gross energy consumption counts all energy before conversion, including nuclear fuel and the water driving hydropower, about 1,000 PJ a year. Only hydropower, wood, waste and other renewables are produced at home; oil, gas and nuclear fuel are all imported. Net imports have covered about 80 % of gross consumption since 1980, falling to about 70 % in recent years.

Final energy consumption

Final consumption is the energy delivered to users, after conversion and transport losses: about 780 PJ in 2025. Oil products, mostly transport fuels and heating oil, are still the largest carrier.

Greenhouse gas emissions

From the national greenhouse gas inventory (FOEN). Emissions fell by about a quarter between 1990 and 2024, mostly in buildings, as oil heating is replaced. Transport is now the largest sector. International aviation, about 5.5 Mt CO2-eq in 2024, is reported separately and not included here.
