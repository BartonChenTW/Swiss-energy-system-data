Energy system studies

Studies that model how Switzerland's energy system could reach net zero by 2050 and keep its electricity supply secure: which models they use, what they find, and whether their results can be reused as data.

On this page: Model comparisonStudiesModelsData gaps

Same scenario, nine models

The SWEET programme's CROSS project gave Swiss modelling teams four harmonised net-zero scenarios: CO2 offset at home or abroad, with or without an electricity agreement with the EU. Its open results show how far models differ even with the same assumptions. In the scenario all nine ran (offsetting abroad, integrated with the EU), solar supplies between 9 and 42 TWh in 2050, wind between 0 and 11 TWh, and net imports between 1 and 18 TWh.
